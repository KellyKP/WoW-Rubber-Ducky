# Changelog

## 0.21.0

First public release.

- **Sound focus:** effects up and everything else to a mumble while your line is out. It fades in and out, and fades back the moment you catch or miss.
- **Bobber marker:** 24 bobber icons, the rubber duck by default, plus sparkles. The icons are previewed in a picker.
- **Fishing camera:** eases in toward the water and returns when you loot.
- **One-button fishing:** cast and reel in with Interact or a controller trigger. The trigger names follow your controller type.
- **Auto loot:** your catch goes straight to your bags, whichever button you use.
- **Catch splash:** a banner with your catch, each line tinted by rarity, and an optional fanfare. Miss splashes ("Missed!" and "Zzz...") come with their own sound.
- **Pole swap:** a draggable macro that swaps your fishing pole and your weapon, including in combat.
- **Welcome screen** on first login, and a settings window with a page for each feature.
